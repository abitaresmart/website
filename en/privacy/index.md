## Who to contact

The AbitareSmart website is available at `abitaresmart.com`. For privacy-related questions, email [info@abitaresmart.com](mailto:info@abitaresmart.com).

## Data involved

This site does not require registration or an account. If you contact us by email, we process your address, message, and any information you choose to send so that we can reply and continue the conversation.

The hosting provider may record essential technical data—such as IP address, date and time, requested page, user agent, and errors—for service security, reliability, and diagnosis.

## External services

Pages may load typefaces from Google Fonts. The site also links to external services such as Instagram; their own privacy notices apply when you follow those links.

## Purpose and retention

We use received data to answer enquiries, consider a possible engagement, protect the site, and meet applicable obligations. Correspondence is retained for as long as needed to manage the relationship and any administrative or legal requirements.

## Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or object to processing, and ask about data portability. You may also contact the relevant supervisory authority.

## Updates

We may revise this notice when the website or its services change. The date above identifies the current version.
