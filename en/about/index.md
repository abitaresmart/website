## We begin with people

We do not sell the same package to every household. We listen to how you use the space, what you want to make easier, and which compromises you do not want to make. Only then do we choose platforms, protocols, and devices.

Our work combines technical depth with care for the everyday experience. Good automation should be dependable, understandable, and reversible. It should work for the person who loves technology and the guest who simply wants to press a switch.

## Independent of brands

We design open, coherent systems and select the right tools for each project. We can integrate Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, Home Assistant, Matter, Thread, Zigbee, and existing devices when they remain a sound choice.

The goal is not to fill a house with products. It is to make the necessary parts work well together, reduce points of failure, and leave a foundation that can evolve.

## Secure from the outset

Home automation touches data, habits, and important physical functions. That is why the network, access, updates, local processing, and continuity are considered during design. Privacy is not a mode to enable later.

## A relationship that continues

A system changes once people live with it. After installation, we review real behaviour, refine scenes and automations, and remain available for maintenance, updates, and new needs.
