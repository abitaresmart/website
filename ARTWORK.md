# Artwork provenance

The smart-home source artwork at `assets/img/brand/abitaresmart-smart-home-artwork.png` was generated from scratch with Codex's built-in image-generation tool. It did not use the Portale logo or either previous website icon as an image reference.

`tools/brand/prepare_smart_home_artwork.py` applies the rounded-square layout separately and creates the preserved 1024 px campaign master at `assets/img/brand/abitaresmart-premium-icon.png`. It does not modify the active navbar or favicon assets.

> Use case: stylized-concept
>
> Asset type: original premium smart-home website icon artwork, square master image before rounded-square layout treatment

The result should feel warm, aspirational, sustainable, clever, and premium—not like a corporate logo and not like a generic stock smart-home symbol.
>
> Scene/backdrop: A vivid, full-bleed miniature world at a magical warm sunrise. The smart home sits slightly elevated among sculpted botanical leaves and soft architectural terrain. Sweeping energy ribbons, tiny connected nodes, atmospheric brush streaks, and restrained sky shapes wrap around the home and create motion, depth, and a sense that the entire house is intelligently connected. The environment must remain readable and uncluttered at icon size.
>
> Subject: One distinctive contemporary home in a friendly three-quarter view, with deep botanical-ink roof and structure, warm canvas and white walls, softly illuminated electric-lime windows and doorway accents, mint connected-energy paths, a single polished coral hub at the heart of the composition, and a few restrained sky-blue system details. The home should feel inhabitable, welcoming, and architecturally refined, with clear roof, doorway, windows, terraces, and subtle smart-home control details. No people.

> Style/medium: Exceptionally polished hand-crafted 2.5D illustrated icon artwork with the richness of a flagship mobile-game icon. Dense sculpted painterly facets, tactile enamel-like surfaces, visible but refined brushwork, crisp silhouettes, layered atmospheric depth, premium editorial charm, sophisticated European design, beautiful micro-detail, and excellent readability. Not photorealism, not flat vector art, not a smooth generic 3D render.

Keep all critical architecture, the coral hub, and the main energy connections inside the central 72 percent so a rounded-square crop can be applied later without losing content. The connected paths should feel organically integrated into the architecture and landscape, never like a pasted-on diagram.

No text, letters, numbers, wordmark, existing logo, watermark, UI, screenshot frame, device mockup, external rounded-corner mask, app-store frame, giant Wi-Fi glyph, padlock glyph, robot, human character, floating phone, generic circuit-board house outline, or multiple houses. ## Premium architectural brand icon

The navbar/favicon source at `assets/img/brand/abitaresmart-logo-artwork.png` was generated from scratch with the built-in image-generation tool. The final direction intentionally avoids both the childish flat-glyph extreme and the elaborate futuristic-illustration extreme. The wordmark is absent from the image and rendered as live HTML beside the mark. `tools/brand/prepare_brand_logo.py` produces the rounded navbar, favicon, Apple touch, and PWA assets.

### Final generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept
>
> Asset type: balanced premium brand icon for the AbitareSmart website navbar, favicon, Apple touch icon, and PWA icon
>
> Primary request: Create one mature, distinctive brand icon for AbitareSmart, an Italian smart-home company. Find the exact middle ground between a childish flat glyph and an elaborate futuristic illustration. The result should feel quietly expensive, considered, architectural, warm, and contemporary—like identity work commissioned from a respected European branding studio. It will sit beside a separately rendered “AbitareSmart” wordmark, so include no text.
>
> Concept: Design a refined “smart threshold” emblem. On a deep Botanical Ink field, center one compact architectural portal made from broad, beautifully proportioned shapes: a warm-white outer form with a softly gabled top, a deep Ink recessed arched doorway, one calm electric-lime semicircular lunetta near the top of the opening, and one small coral hub inset near the lower center. Use one restrained mint reveal or edge detail to suggest connection. The shapes must feel intentionally related and ownable, not assembled from stock home-automation symbols.
>
> Scene/background: Full-bleed Botanical Ink #0B211F with a barely perceptible Ink-soft #17302D tonal falloff and fine natural grain. No scenery, room, garden, landscape, sky, orbit, particles, circuitry, or decorative technology. Do not bake in rounded corners; the rounded-square crop will be applied afterward.
>
> Style/medium: Premium architectural brand object rendered in restrained shallow 2.5D relief. Crisp custom geometry, sophisticated optical balance, satin lacquer background, honed warm ceramic portal, softly translucent lime enamel, and polished coral inset. Use subtle material variation and impeccable edges, but keep surfaces calm. The icon should look designed and art-directed, not like clip art, an emoji, a toy, or a miniature illustration.
>
> Detail level: Medium-low visual complexity with high execution quality. Approximately five large visual components. No micro-scenes or tiny decorative details. Premium comes from proportion, negative space, material restraint, edge quality, and lighting—not from adding objects.
>
> Composition/framing: Front-on, centered, near-symmetrical composition with slight optical asymmetry only if it improves balance. The complete emblem occupies about 62 percent of the canvas, with generous breathing room. Thick, favicon-safe forms; clear at 16 pixels and elegant at 48–180 pixels. The portal must have a memorable silhouette without becoming a generic house-outline icon.
>
> Lighting/mood: Soft controlled studio light from upper left, a restrained warm highlight along one architectural edge, delicate recessed shadow inside the doorway, and tiny enamel highlights on lime and coral. Calm, welcoming, intelligent, secure, human, sustainable, and premium. No dramatic rim glow, cinematic rays, neon, lens flare, or deep sci-fi lighting.
>
> Color palette: Use only the AbitareSmart palette: Botanical Ink #0B211F for the field and deepest recess; Ink soft #17302D for subtle depth; Canvas #F7F4EC, Surface #EFEADF, and White #FFFDF8 for the architectural form; Electric Primary #DDF86A and Primary hover #EBFF98 for the lunetta; Primary pale #D8F7E8 or Secondary soft #AEECCF for one restrained reveal; Coral Accent #FF735C and Accent strong #B93A31 for the hub. No colors outside this palette.
>
> Materials/textures: Satin botanical lacquer, honed warm ceramic, softly translucent lime enamel, polished coral inlay, extremely subtle paper-like micrograin. No glossy plastic, chrome, glass architecture, metallic machinery, painterly mosaic facets, or visible brush-stroke atmosphere.
>
> Constraints: One icon only; no text, letters, initials, numbers, wordmark, watermark, existing logo, multiple concepts, logo sheet, device mockup, realistic house, detailed building, roof tiles, windows, chimney, furniture, people, plants, landscape, Wi-Fi glyph, circuit traces, toggle switch, padlock, shield, key, plug, lightbulb, robot, phone, energy ribbon, orbit, floating nodes, UI, or baked rounded-corner mask.
>
> Avoid: childish proportions, cartoon styling, stock home icon, simplistic clip art, cheap flat vector appearance, oversized bubbly shapes, excessive empty space, futuristic technology, sci-fi portal, complex illustration, painterly scene, hyper-detailed materials, dramatic perspective, generic 3D render, random arc-and-dot composition.
